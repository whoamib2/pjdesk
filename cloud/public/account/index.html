PJDesk account

Your account turns on Cloud Sync: an encrypted copy of your PJDesk Server, so a backup server can take over.

Your password never leaves your browser or your server; PJDesk only receives a key made from it, and your server backups are encrypted with another key only your servers have. That also means PJDesk can't recover a forgotten password, so keep it in your password manager.
